Hyperion in the browser

Your StarCraft Files

Hyperion plays from a copy of StarCraft 1.17's files kept in this browser. Choose the folder it is installed in: the files stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.

The host’s copy comes over this network, about 90 MB, and is kept in this browser afterwards. It has no music or movies: those are in the two optional files below, which your own folder can add.
